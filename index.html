Privacy Policy for TailNest

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Thank you for using TailNest, an offline pet care and health organizer designed for dogs, cats, birds, fish, rabbits, reptiles, and small companions.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

TailNest works completely offline. We do not require user accounts or sign-in, and we do not collect, transmit, track, sell, or share any personal information, pet health logs, photos, location data, or device identifiers with external servers or third parties.

2. Local On-Device Storage & Backups

All pet profiles, vaccination logs, weight records, symptom notes, daily care routines, and expenses are stored locally on your own device. Optional JSON backup exports remain strictly under your own control.

3. Permissions & Android Photo Picker

Pet photos are attached using the zero-permission Android Photo Picker. Broad storage permissions are never requested. Dialing your saved vet, adding calendar reminders, or sharing a Vet PDF happens only when you tap those actions.

4. Third-Party Services & Future Updates

The current offline version contains no third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or ad networks. If advertising (such as Google AdMob) is added in a future release, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety section will be updated prior to release.

5. Children's Privacy

TailNest is intended for general pet owners (13+ / 18+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Veterinary & Educational Disclaimer: TailNest is an organizational and educational reference tool only. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace licensed veterinary care. Always contact a qualified veterinarian in an emergency.
